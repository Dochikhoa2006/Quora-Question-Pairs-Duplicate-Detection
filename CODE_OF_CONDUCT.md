# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free project for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and respectful in technical discussion.
- Critique ideas and implementations, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes openly.
- Respect privacy, dataset terms, and the people represented in data.
- Give appropriate credit for ideas, code, research, and review work.
- Help make project spaces welcoming to newcomers.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or sexualized attention
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption
- Publishing private information without explicit permission
- Deliberately misleading model, benchmark, or authorship claims
- Sharing credentials, private datasets, or harmful content unnecessarily

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and project-related public communication. Report incidents privately to the
repository owner using the contact method in `SECURITY.md`.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and may temporarily or permanently ban
participation when behavior violates this policy. Reports will be handled as
confidentially and fairly as practical.
